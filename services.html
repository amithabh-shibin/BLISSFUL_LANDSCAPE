Landscape design & construction

Plan and build indoor and outdoor spaces around how you use the property. Discuss layout, planting, circulation and finishing for villa gardens, courtyards and commercial grounds.

Garden & landscape maintenance

Keep planted areas presentable with care tailored to your garden. Discuss pruning, plant care, lawn upkeep and the recurring attention your site needs.

Mention the plants and areas you want maintained, how often they need attention and any specific issues. Recent photos help us understand the condition of lawn edges, beds and hedges.

Indoor & outdoor plant supply

Source plants for interior displays, terraces and outdoor beds. Share the light conditions, space and look you want so the discussion can focus on suitable options.

For indoor plants, note the nearest windows and the hours of direct light. For outdoor plants, include the planting area, sun exposure and your preferred height or coverage.

Natural grass supply

Enquire about natural grass for villa lawns, outdoor common areas and garden upgrades. Share the area in square metres, usage and site conditions to help scope your request.

Send the lawn dimensions and, if available, a photo of the ground and existing irrigation. Let us know if you are planning a new lawn, replacing worn turf or filling small patches.

Irrigation materials supply

Discuss irrigation materials for new landscapes or existing gardens. Send a component list, photos or system details to help identify the materials you need.

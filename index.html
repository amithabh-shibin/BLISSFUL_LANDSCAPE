FAQ

Landscaping questions, answered.

Do you design and build new gardens in Dubai?

Yes. Blissful Landscape works on indoor and outdoor spaces, from layout and planting discussions through landscape construction. Send photos and approximate dimensions for an initial conversation.

Can I enquire about maintenance for an existing garden?

Yes. Share the location, approximate size and any concerns such as overgrowth, patchy lawn or plants that need attention. We can discuss a suitable maintenance scope.

Can I buy plants, natural grass or irrigation materials separately?

Yes. The company supplies indoor and outdoor plants, natural grass and irrigation materials. Include quantities, sizes or a material list where possible.

How do I request a landscaping quote?

Use the contact form to send an enquiry on WhatsApp, or email us with the Dubai location, photos, dimensions and the work you have in mind. Pricing depends on the agreed scope and site requirements.

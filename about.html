HORTICULTURE & GARDEN CARE

Plan for the conditions on site.

Sun exposure, wind and irrigation access affect how a garden develops. We use those details to discuss where to place plants, how they may fill out, and what upkeep they will need through hotter months.

For an existing garden, share photos of areas that dry out quickly, stay shaded or need frequent trimming. This helps define the work before a maintenance scope is agreed.

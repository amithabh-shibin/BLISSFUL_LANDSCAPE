BLISSFUL LANDSCAPE WEBSITE

Preview
Extract the ZIP and open index.html. All images, styles and scripts are included.

Publish
Upload index.html, about.html, services.html, gallery.html, contact.html,
robots.txt, sitemap.xml and the assets folder to the website document root.
Keep the folder structure and filenames intact. Use HTTPS.

SEO
The canonical URLs, social image URLs, structured data and sitemap use
https://www.blissfullandscape.com, taken from the supplied business card.
Confirm that this is the production HTTPS URL. If the preferred hostname
differs, update it wherever used and redirect alternate hostnames. Submit
https://www.blissfullandscape.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console once live.

Customer reviews and imagery
The testimonial cards contain fictional sample reviews and generated portraits.
Replace the sample cards with approved customer quotes and photos to publish
genuine testimonials. The Google Maps link leads to the company's listing.

Client-supplied project images appear in the slideshow and gallery. Other
garden photos illustrate services. The About page links to a high-resolution
image rendered from the supplied ISO certificate.

Contact
The quote form opens WhatsApp with the entered details. Visitors tap Send
in WhatsApp. The separate WhatsApp links and email address are also
available. Only the city and country were supplied on the card.
